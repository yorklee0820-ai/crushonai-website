How the credit system actually works

This is the part most people get wrong, so read it before subscribing. CrushOn AI separates access from consumption.

  • Free models never consume credits. You can chat all day on Crushon Mira, GLM 4.6 and Crushon Leo without spending anything, on any tier including free.
  • Pro models consume credits from a monthly allowance. The free tier includes roughly 100 credits per month to test them.
  • Ultra models consume credits faster and require a paid plan. This is where heavy users burn through an allowance in days.
  • The Chat Package is additive. It grants more credits but does not itself unlock Ultra models — you still need a qualifying plan.

Practical consequence: if you are happy with base models, CrushOn AI costs you nothing forever. If you want Claude Opus 4.7 or GPT 5.2 every day, budget for credits on top of your subscription — that combined figure is your real monthly cost.

Which plan should you pick?

  1. Start free. Unlimited base-model chat is enough to decide whether you like the platform. The only real pain is the 7-day history wipe.
  2. Standard ($5.99) if your reason to pay is persistence and group chat rather than model quality. This is the cheapest fix for losing your history.
  3. Premium annually if you want the Ultra models. It is the deepest genuine discount and the tier most reviewers land on.
  4. Luxe and above only if you need custom voices, adjustable reply length and the 24K context ceiling for long-running stories.

Annual billing: check the maths

Annual billing is usually cheaper, but not every advertised discount is what it appears to be. Independent reviewers found that the Premium annual badge (~47% off) and Luxe (~37%) hold up against the monthly price, while some other tiers advertise savings larger than the real difference, and certain bundle configurations save nothing at all. Before you commit for a year, divide the annual total by twelve and compare it to the monthly rate — that is the only number that matters.

Hidden costs and gotchas

  • Free history expires. Seven days of inactivity and the conversation is gone. This is the sharpest edge on the free plan.
  • Reply length is capped on free at roughly 225 tokens, which limits dense prose.
  • One chat per character on the free tier — no parallel scenes with the same character.
  • Credits do not roll over in the way people assume. Treat them as a monthly budget, not a balance.
  • No refunds are guaranteed — check the current terms before buying a year.

Pricing FAQ

Is CrushOn AI free?

Yes, indefinitely. Unlimited messages on free models, 8K memory, 200 stored messages per character and 100 monthly Pro credits. History clears after 7 days idle.

Why do I need credits if I already pay?

Pro and Ultra models consume credits in addition to your plan fee. Free models never do — staying on base models means never thinking about credits.

Is annual billing worth it?

Generally yes on Premium (~47% saving). Verify the monthly-equivalent yourself, since not every advertised discount matches the real difference.

What is the Chat Package?

An add-on granting extra message credits for advanced models. It works alongside a plan and does not replace one.

Does the free tier really have unlimited messages?

On free models, yes — there is no daily cap. The limits are on depth: memory, reply length, stored messages and history retention.

Can I use a virtual card?

Yes, and it is worth doing if you would rather not see the charge descriptor on your main statement.

Decide with real usage, not a price table

The free tier is enough to tell you whether model depth is worth paying for. Start there.
