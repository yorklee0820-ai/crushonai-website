Is CrushOn AI safe?

Short answer: it is safe enough for adults who take basic precautions, and its privacy claims are the weakest part of the product. Here is exactly what is claimed, what is verified, and what to do about the gap. 18+

What CrushOn AI claims

  • Conversations are private to your account.
  • Chats are not used to train models.
  • Mature content sits behind an NSFW toggle and an age declaration.
  • Account deletion is available.

What independent reviewers found

Privacy is the most criticised area in every third-party review we consulted — it consistently scores lowest of any category, below character quality, models and value. Reviewers note extensive personal data collection as a con, and several advise practical precautions rather than trust.

The specific gap: no independent audit. The claims above are the platform’s own statements, not verified by a third party. That is not unusual in this category, but it is the reason to be deliberate.

The precautions that cost you nothing

  1. Use a separate email address. A burner address for this account costs nothing and limits correlation with your identity elsewhere.
  2. Never share identifying details. No real name, address, workplace, phone number or photos of yourself. Nothing in a roleplay requires them.
  3. Assume persistence. Treat anything you type as stored. If you would not want it in a database, do not type it.
  4. Use a virtual card if you would rather not see the charge descriptor on your main statement.
  5. Remember the free tier wipes history after 7 days idle — which is a privacy benefit if you want minimal retention, and an annoyance if you do not.

Adults only. CrushOn AI hosts unfiltered mature content and is not suitable for minors under any circumstances. If you are not of legal age in your jurisdiction, do not use it. All characters are fictional.

Is it a scam?

No. It is a functioning product with a large active user base, a public community and a long operating history. The complaints that do appear are about the credit system, deleted free-tier history and inconsistent character quality — not about non-delivery. See our full review for the balanced picture.

When it is the wrong choice

If you need independently audited privacy guarantees, this is not the platform for you, and no amount of hygiene changes that. In that case look at mainstream filtered services with published compliance documentation, accepting that you lose unfiltered roleplay. Our alternatives list covers the trade-offs.

FAQ

Is CrushOn AI safe to use?

For adults taking basic precautions, yes. Chats are private to your account and not used for training per the platform — but those claims are unaudited.

Does it store my conversations?

Yes, to deliver the service and support memory. Assume anything you type persists.

Are privacy claims audited?

No. That is the single biggest gap and the main reason reviewers mark privacy down.

Is it safe for minors?

No. Adults-only, 18+ or the legal age in your jurisdiction.

Can I delete my data?

Yes via account settings. Export anything you want to keep first — deletion is irreversible.
