Memory is the whole comparison

CrushOn AI memory is a context window: the character sees what fits inside it, and older content falls out. Tiers scale that window from 8K to 24K, and paid plans retain history instead of deleting it after seven days of inactivity. Within a thread it is good; across weeks it is adequate.

Nomi AI treats memory as a first-class feature rather than a window size. It maintains relationship continuity — what you told it, what happened, how the dynamic has evolved — across sessions in a way that context-window systems do not. If you run one long relationship rather than many standalone scenes, that difference is the entire decision. Our memory guide explains the mechanics.

What you give up by switching to Nomi AI

  • Library breadth. CrushOn AI’s 8M+ community cards dwarf Nomi AI’s selection.
  • Model choice. No equivalent to 13+ switchable models.
  • Group chat. Multi-character scenes are a CrushOn AI feature.
  • Price. Around $8.33/month annually versus a genuinely usable free tier.

What you gain

  • Recall that survives weeks. Details you established early are still there later.
  • Continuity without recaps. Less need to manually remind the character of plot state.
  • Fewer context-window workarounds. No strategic summarising every 50 messages.

Verdict

Stay with CrushOn AI if you want variety, model control, group scenes and the lowest cost — and you are willing to recap occasionally. Move to Nomi AI if you maintain one long-running relationship and constant forgetting ruins it for you. The price difference is small; the memory difference is the point.

Before you switch: try the Cheaper fixes first. Upgrading CrushOn AI to a 16K or 24K tier, keeping sessions under ~100 messages and recapping deliberately solves most forgetting at a fraction of the cost of switching platforms.
