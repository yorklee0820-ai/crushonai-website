CrushOn AI NSFW characters guide

How the NSFW toggle works, how to find mature characters that are actually worth chatting with, which models handle explicit roleplay best, and how to create your own. 18+

CrushOn AI is an adults-only platform that allows NSFW character roleplay. The NSFW toggle in your account settings controls whether mature and explicit characters appear in your library search results. When it is off, you see only safe-for-work content. When it is on, the full library is available — romantic, explicit, dark, and everything in between.

This guide covers how the content system actually works, how to find NSFW characters that produce good output (most do not), which models handle mature roleplay best, and how to create private characters that match exactly what you want.

How the NSFW toggle works

The toggle is in your account settings, not buried in a menu. When you first sign up, NSFW content is off by default — you see the safe-for-work portion of the library. Enabling it takes one click and an age confirmation. Once enabled, NSFW-tagged characters appear in search results, category browsing and recommendations.

Key points:

  • The toggle is per-account, not per-conversation. You cannot NSFW-filter one chat and unfilter another.
  • Disabling the toggle hides NSFW characters from browse and search but does not delete existing conversations with those characters.
  • Character creators tag their own characters as SFW or NSFW when publishing. The platform does not auto-tag.
  • You can create characters of either type regardless of your toggle setting — the toggle only affects what you see in the library.

Finding quality NSFW characters

The NSFW section of the library is large and mostly mediocre. This is not a CrushOn AI problem — it is a user-generated content problem. Most NSFW characters are defined with generic descriptions and no example dialogue, which means the model has no behavioural reference and falls back on stock responses. Finding quality requires filtering aggressively.

The four quality signals (same as SFW, but more important here)

  1. Example dialogue quality. Even more critical for NSFW characters because the model needs specific behavioural cues for intimate scenes. If the example dialogue shows distinct voice, emotional range and scene-specific reactions, the character will perform well. If it is generic, the output will be too.
  2. Conversation count. Popular NSFW cards have been refined through hundreds of conversations. The creator has usually updated the card based on how it actually performs, not just how they imagined it would.
  3. Scenario specificity. “You are alone together in your apartment after a party” gives the model far more to work with than “you meet a beautiful stranger.”
  4. Personality depth beyond the physical. The best NSFW characters have personalities, backstories and emotional dynamics — not just physical descriptions. A character who is confident in public but vulnerable alone produces better roleplay than one who is simply described as attractive.

Search strategies

  • Use specific tags, not just “NSFW.” Search by relationship dynamic (romantic, dominant, submissive), character archetype (tsundere, villain, mentor) or scenario type (slice of life, fantasy, sci-fi).
  • Sort by popularity or conversation count rather than newest. New cards are untested; popular cards have been iterated.
  • Read the full card before starting. Characters with detailed backstories and five or more example dialogues produce dramatically better output than thin cards.
  • Audition in the first five messages. Send an off-script opening and see how the character adapts. If it falls back on generic lines, move on.

Creating private NSFW characters

Building your own character is often faster than searching, and the result is tuned to exactly what you want. For NSFW characters specifically, a few extra principles apply:

  1. Set visibility to private. When creating the character, choose private visibility. Only you can see and chat with it. You can always change it to public later if you want to share.
  2. Write the personality before the physical description. Personality drives dialogue quality. Physical descriptions drive the first message only. A character who is “intellectually competitive, uses humour as deflection, secretly insecure about their writing” will produce better roleplay than one described only in physical terms.
  3. Include five or more example dialogues. This is the single most impactful field for NSFW characters. Show the character reacting to different emotional registers: playful, vulnerable, assertive, surprised, intimate. The more varied the examples, the more range the model has to draw from.
  4. Define boundaries in the character, not in system prompts. If the character has specific preferences, limits or dynamics, write them into the personality and backstory fields. The model reads these as character traits and follows them more consistently than external instructions.
  5. Set a concrete opening scenario. Place the character in a specific situation with emotional context. “It is raining, you are both stuck in a bookshop after closing time, and the character just found out they did not get the job they wanted” gives the model more to work with than any amount of physical description.

Privacy and safety

CrushOn AI states that conversations are private to your account and are not used for model training. However, these claims have not been independently audited — this is the most common criticism in third-party reviews. For NSFW content specifically, additional hygiene is worth practising:

  • Use a dedicated email address that does not contain your real name.
  • Do not share real personal details — name, address, workplace, photos — in conversations.
  • Treat anything you type as stored permanently, even if the platform says otherwise.
  • Use private characters for anything you do not want associated with a public profile.
  • Use a virtual card for payments if you do not want the charge descriptor on your main bank statement.

Memory and long-form NSFW roleplay

For short sessions, memory does not matter much. For long-running storylines — the kind that span days or weeks — memory is the most important feature. CrushOn AI's tiered memory system (8K free, 16K Standard, 24K Ultra) means that on the free tier, characters start forgetting details from earlier in the conversation after about fifty messages. On Ultra, they can recall details from a hundred or more messages back.

If you are building a long-form NSFW storyline, the Standard tier at minimum is worth considering for the memory upgrade alone. The 16K context window keeps characters consistent through significantly longer arcs.

FAQ

How do I enable NSFW characters?

Account settings → NSFW toggle. One click plus an age confirmation. The toggle is per-account and affects library visibility only.

Are my NSFW conversations private?

The platform states conversations are private and not used for training. This has not been independently audited. Use a separate email and do not share real personal details.

Which model is best for NSFW roleplay?

Claude Opus 4.7 for depth and nuance, Crushon Mochi V3 for the best free-tier experience. Model choice matters less than character quality.

Can I create private NSFW characters?

Yes. Set visibility to private when creating the character. Only you can see and chat with private characters.

Is CrushOn AI the only unfiltered platform?

No. Janitor AI, Muah AI, SpicyChat AI and others also allow unfiltered content. CrushOn AI stands out for its model variety and free tier.

How much memory do NSFW characters get?

Same as all characters: 8K on free, 16K on Standard, up to 24K on Ultra. Memory tier determines how far back the character can recall details.
