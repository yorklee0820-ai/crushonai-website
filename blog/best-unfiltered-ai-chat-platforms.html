7 best unfiltered AI chat platforms

Every platform that lets you roleplay without content filters — compared on model choice, memory, character libraries, pricing and what they actually let you write. 18+

Unfiltered AI chat means the platform does not insert safety refusals into your roleplay. You write a scene — romantic, violent, morally grey, explicit — and the AI plays its role without breaking character to deliver a disclaimer. That is the defining feature. Everything else — model count, memory depth, pricing — is secondary to whether the platform actually stays out of your way.

The term has become a marketing keyword, and not every platform that claims to be “unfiltered” delivers on that promise consistently. Some are unfiltered on specific models only, others filter quietly in the background, and a few genuinely do not interfere. This guide sorts the real from the aspirational.

1. CrushOn AI — best overall for unfiltered roleplay

CrushOn AI is built around one idea: adult character roleplay without safety interjections. The NSFW toggle is user-controlled, the library has 8M+ community-created characters, and you can switch between 13+ models mid-conversation. The free tier does not cap messages on base models — you can chat as long as you want without paying.

What sets it apart from the others on this list is model depth. Most unfiltered platforms run a single proprietary model or ask you to bring your own API key. CrushOn AI gives you Claude Opus 4.7, GPT 5.2, DeepSeek R1, GLM 5.1 and several others, all pre-configured. Memory reaches 24K on the Ultra tier, which means characters remember details from a hundred messages back.

The main drawback: Pro and Ultra models consume monthly credits, and the credit system is the most common complaint in third-party reviews. If you want unlimited access to the best models, you pay per message on top of your subscription.

2. Janitor AI — best for bring-your-own-key users

Janitor AI takes a different approach: it provides the character interface and you supply the AI backend via an OpenAI or similar API key. That makes it genuinely unfiltered — whatever your API key allows, the platform allows. The character library is large and the interface is straightforward.

The trade-off is complexity and cost. You need to understand API billing, configure your key, and manage rate limits yourself. The actual cost per message depends on which model you use through your key, and casual users often spend more than they expect. There is no native voice, no group chat, and the mobile experience is web-only.

Best for: users who already have API keys and want full control over which model runs their chat.

3. Candy AI — best for image-rich companion experience

Candy AI targets a different need: visual polish. The platform generates AI images of your companion, offers voice messages, and has a more polished onboarding flow than most competitors. Roleplay is mostly unfiltered on the companion side, though the image generation has its own content policies.

Where it falls short compared to CrushOn AI: fewer models (proprietary backend), weaker memory, and the free tier is heavily restricted. At $12.99/month it costs more than CrushOn AI's Standard plan while offering less model variety. The visual experience is the real differentiator — if you want images alongside text, Candy AI does that better.

4. Nomi AI — best for long-term emotional memory

Nomi AI's standout feature is memory quality. Nomis form persistent memories of your interactions and reference them unprompted in future conversations. The emotional depth is higher than most competitors — your Nomi will remember that you mentioned a bad day at work three weeks ago and bring it up naturally.

The platform is not explicitly “unfiltered” in the same way CrushOn AI is. It allows mature content but applies light content guidelines. The free tier lets you have one Nomi; additional Nomis and features require a subscription starting at $15.99/month. There is no model switching — you use Nomi's proprietary model.

Best for: users who prioritise emotional continuity and relationship depth over raw model choice.

5. Muah AI — best for multimedia chat

Muah AI combines text chat with photo exchange, voice messages and phone calls. The unfiltered stance is genuine — the platform does not censor roleplay content. It positions itself as more of an AI companion than a character-roleplay platform.

Pricing starts at $19.99/month, which is the highest on this list. The free tier is very limited. The character library is smaller than CrushOn AI's, and model details are less transparent. If you want a multimedia companion experience with real-time voice and photos, Muah AI is the most complete option; if you want deep text roleplay, others do it better for less money.

6. SpicyChat AI — best free browsing experience

SpicyChat AI has a large community character library and a browsing experience that works well without paying. The queue-based free tier lets you chat with characters, though with slower response times during peak hours. Paid tiers remove the queue and unlock additional features.

Content policy is mostly unfiltered for text roleplay. The platform runs several model options but does not match CrushOn AI's model count or switching flexibility. At $9.99/month for the basic paid tier, it is cheaper than most competitors, but you get what you pay for in terms of memory depth and reply quality.

Best for: casual users who want to browse a large character library without committing to a subscription.

7. CraveU AI — best budget unfiltered option

CraveU AI is a newer entrant focused on being straightforward: unfiltered chat, multiple model options, and a clean interface. Pricing starts at $9.99/month. The character library is growing but still smaller than the established platforms. Memory and model quality are adequate but not category-leading.

Best for: users who want an unfiltered experience at a lower price point and do not need the deepest memory or the largest library.

How to choose

The right platform depends on what you actually want to do:

  • Deep text roleplay with model choice → CrushOn AI (13+ models, 24K memory, generous free tier)
  • Full control over the AI backend → Janitor AI (BYOK, any model you have a key for)
  • Visual companion with images → Candy AI (AI-generated images, polished UI)
  • Emotional continuity and memory → Nomi AI (strongest long-term memory quality)
  • Multimedia with voice and photos → Muah AI (phone calls, photo exchange)
  • Free browsing with no commitment → SpicyChat AI (large library, queue-based free tier)

None of these platforms have been independently audited for privacy. Every one of them stores your conversations on their servers. Use a separate email address, do not share real identifying information, and treat anything you type as persistent regardless of what the privacy policy says.

What about Character.AI?

Character.AI is the most popular AI character chat platform, but it is not unfiltered. The platform enforces strict content policies and will refuse, redirect or break character during mature roleplay. If that filtering is what you are trying to avoid, every platform on this list is a better fit. If you want a polished, family-safe experience with the largest user base, Character.AI is still the market leader — just not for this use case.

FAQ

What is unfiltered AI chat?

Platforms that do not enforce content safety filters on character roleplay. You can write mature, dark, or explicit fictional scenarios without the AI refusing or inserting disclaimers.

Are unfiltered AI chat platforms safe to use?

Safe in terms of account security, but privacy claims vary. None have been independently audited. Use a separate email and avoid sharing real personal details.

Which one is free?

CrushOn AI has the most generous free tier — unlimited messages on base models with no daily cap. Janitor AI is free if you bring your own API key.

Is Character.AI unfiltered?

No. Character.AI enforces strict content filters and will refuse or redirect mature roleplay.

Can I use these platforms on mobile?

CrushOn AI has an Android APK and a responsive web app. Most others are web-app only. None of the unfiltered platforms have iOS App Store listings due to Apple's content policies.
