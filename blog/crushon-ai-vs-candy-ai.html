The deciding factor: do you want images?

CrushOn AI is entirely text-based. It has voice replies but generates no visuals of any kind, and that is not changing. Candy AI generates images of your companion as a core feature. If pictures are the point, the comparison ends here — CrushOn AI cannot serve you.

If pictures are not the point, CrushOn AI is the better value: a vastly larger library, 13+ models you can switch mid-conversation, group chat, and a free tier that genuinely lets you chat indefinitely on base models.

Where Candy AI clearly wins

  • Visual content. Images of your character, which CrushOn AI does not offer at all.
  • Interface. Cleaner, more intuitive, less cluttered navigation.
  • Consistency. Replies are more reliably good; CrushOn AI quality swings hard depending on which card you picked.
  • Less card-hunting. A curated library means less time sorting good characters from bad.

Where CrushOn AI clearly wins

  • Price and free tier. Unlimited base-model messages for $0 is unmatched in this category.
  • Library size. 8M+ cards versus a curated selection.
  • Model choice. 13+ models, switchable mid-chat — Candy AI gives you one.
  • Group chat with three or four characters in a scene.
  • Creator freedom for characters and scenarios a curated platform would not host.

Verdict

Choose CrushOn AI if you are text-first, want maximum variety and model control, and prefer not to pay until a feature genuinely matters. Choose Candy AI if visuals matter, you value polish and consistency over breadth, and you are willing to pay roughly a dollar more per month for it. Many users run both: CrushOn AI for long-form roleplay, Candy AI when they want images.

Also consider Nomi AI if memory is your priority, or our full alternatives shortlist.

FAQ

Is Candy AI better than CrushOn AI?

For images, polish and consistency, yes. For price, library size and model variety, CrushOn AI is better.

Does CrushOn AI generate images?

No. Text and voice only — no image generation.

Which is cheaper?

CrushOn AI, especially on the free tier where base-model messages are unlimited.

Can I use both?

Yes — a common setup is CrushOn AI for long-form roleplay and Candy AI for visuals.
