About crushonai.website

An independent guide to CrushOn AI — reviews, pricing breakdowns and practical how-tos. 18+

What this site is

crushonai.website is an independent publication covering one platform: CrushOn AI. We test it, document how its features actually behave, explain the pricing and credit system, and compare it honestly against the alternatives.

We are not affiliated with CrushOn AI and do not operate the platform. We cannot access your account, process refunds or resolve technical faults — for those, contact the platform directly.

What we cover

  • Reviews — hands-on testing with scores and named limitations. See the CrushOn AI review.
  • Pricing analysis — the plan ladder, annual billing and the credit system, on the pricing page.
  • Comparisons — vs Character.AI, vs Janitor AI and the full alternatives list.
  • Guides — creating characters, choosing models, memory, mobile and troubleshooting in the blog.

How we stay honest

Every review lists what did not work alongside what did. Where we cite third-party scores or platform claims, we name the source and the date. Where we could not verify something — privacy audits, regional pricing, iOS availability — we say so rather than guessing. Our full methodology is on the editorial policy page.

Adults only

CrushOn AI hosts unfiltered mature content. This site discusses it in practical terms and is intended for adults of legal age in their jurisdiction. All characters referenced are fictional.

Get in touch

Corrections, updates and tips are welcome — accuracy matters more to us than being first. Use the contact page.
