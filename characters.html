How the CrushOn AI character library works

Every character is a “card” created by a community member: a name, a personality description, an opening scenario, example dialogue, a set of tags and — on newer cards — a voice preset. When you start a chat, the model reads that card as its instruction set and plays the role. The better the card, the better the character holds its voice.

Because anyone can publish, breadth is enormous and quality is uneven. Two cards with the same premise can feel like entirely different products. Learning to judge a card in thirty seconds is the highest-value skill on this platform.

How to spot a good character card

  1. Read the example dialogue, not the description. Descriptions are marketing; example dialogue is the actual writing style the model will imitate. If the samples are flat, the chat will be flat.
  2. Check the conversation count. High-volume cards have usually been revised repeatedly by their creators. It is the closest thing to a quality signal in the library.
  3. Look for a specific opening scenario. Cards that drop you into a concrete situation give the model something to work with. Vague premises produce generic replies.
  4. Avoid one-line personalities. A card defined only by “flirty and possessive” will drift within twenty messages.
  5. Sample several before committing. Spend your first session auditioning rather than investing in one character.

Creating your own CrushOn AI character

Building a character is free, and it is worth doing even if you mostly browse — a card tuned to exactly what you want beats an hour of scrolling. The fields that matter most:

  • Personality: write in specific traits and contradictions, not adjectives. “Deflects with humour when cornered” beats “funny and shy.”
  • Scenario: give a concrete situation with stakes. The model needs a starting state.
  • Example dialogue: five or more exchanges. This is the single strongest lever on output quality — it teaches tone, pacing and vocabulary far better than a description can.
  • Speaking style: sentence length, formality, verbal tics, what the character never says.
  • Voice: pick from 40+ presets across nine languages, or design a custom voice on higher tiers.

Publish publicly if you want others to use it, or keep it private. Private characters are the better default while you are still iterating.

Characters FAQ

How many characters does CrushOn AI have?

The library lists 8M+ community-created AI characters, with new cards published daily across dozens of genres.

How do I find good CrushOn AI characters?

Filter by tag and genre, then judge by example dialogue and conversation count rather than the description. See our picks and method.

Can I keep a character private?

Yes. Characters can be published publicly or kept private, and you can change visibility later.

Do characters remember me?

Within the context window, yes — up to 24K on the Ultra tier. Cross-session recall depends on your tier and on whether history is retained; the free tier clears history after 7 days idle.

Can multiple characters appear in one chat?

Yes, group chat supports three to four characters at once and is available from the Standard tier upward.

Do characters have voices?

Many do. 40+ presets across nine languages, switchable mid-conversation, with custom voice design on higher tiers.
