1. Character.AI — the mainstream option

The default choice if content filtering is a feature rather than a bug. Character.AI is more polished than CrushOn AI, has well-built native iOS and Android apps, and enforces strict content rules. You lose mature roleplay entirely and you are locked to a single in-house model, but the interface and reliability are better. Full breakdown: CrushOn AI vs Character.AI.

2. Janitor AI — control via your own API key

Janitor AI allows mature content and is popular with users who want to choose and configure their own language model backend. The trade-off is setup friction: you supply an API key and manage usage yourself, which means unpredictable costs and more tinkering. If you enjoy that, it is powerful; if you want to sign up and chat, CrushOn AI is easier. See CrushOn AI vs Janitor AI.

3. Candy AI — images and polish

The most common upgrade path from CrushOn AI. Candy AI generates AI images of your companion, has a noticeably cleaner interface, and handles conversation more consistently. It costs slightly more but not dramatically so. Choose it if visual content matters — CrushOn AI is entirely text-based and will not change. Full comparison.

4. Nomi AI — the memory specialist

If your complaint about CrushOn AI is that characters forget things across sessions, Nomi AI is the answer. It has the strongest long-term memory in this category and maintains relationship continuity far better. It costs more, and its character library is smaller than CrushOn AI’s. Full comparison.

5. Kindroid — voice and video

Kindroid offers real-time voice calls and video avatars, neither of which CrushOn AI provides. Conversation quality is strong and memory is good. It sits at a higher price point, so it makes sense only if voice is the feature you actually want.

6. Nectar AI — customization at the same price

Similar price to CrushOn AI with deeper character customization tooling. Worth a look if you spend most of your time building characters rather than browsing them, and want more control over how they are defined.

7. Lovescape — broader feature set

A full companion product with features beyond text chat. It is a reasonable step up if you have outgrown a text-only platform but do not specifically need images or calls.

How to choose

  • Want unfiltered chat under $10/month? Stay with CrushOn AI — nothing here beats it on value.
  • Want images? Candy AI.
  • Want real memory? Nomi AI.
  • Want voice or video calls? Kindroid.
  • Want filtered, safe, polished? Character.AI.
  • Want to configure your own model? Janitor AI.

Honest note: if you are happy with text-only unfiltered roleplay, switching away from CrushOn AI usually means paying more for features you may not use. Its free tier is the strongest reason to try it before any alternative.

Still comparing?

Start with the free tier and test the specific thing you care about. You will know quickly whether you need an alternative.
