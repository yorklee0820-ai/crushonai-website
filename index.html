What is CrushOn AI?

CrushOn AI is an AI character chat platform where you hold open-ended conversations with fictional personas — original characters, anime archetypes, fantasy heroes, or companions you design yourself. It exists for one specific job: unfiltered character roleplay. That is the whole product. There is no productivity mode, no code assistant, no document editing.

The distinction matters because it explains everything else about the platform. Mainstream assistants like ChatGPT or Claude are tuned to be helpful, harmless and honest, which in practice means they refuse a large slice of fictional writing — a jealous villain's threat, a romantic scene, a morally grey antagonist. CrushOn AI removes that layer. You can write a scene and the character plays it back without a safety interjection, because the platform treats adult fictional roleplay as the intended use case rather than an abuse case.

Three things define the experience in practice. First, the character library: millions of community-created characters with defined personalities, backstories, opening scenarios and example dialogue, new ones published daily. Second, model choice: instead of one black-box backend, you pick from 13+ models — free, Pro and Ultra tiers — and can switch mid-conversation when a scene needs a different voice. Third, memory: characters retain context across sessions, up to 24K working memory on the top tier, so a plot thread you set up three weeks ago still lands.

CrushOn AI is free to start. The free tier is unusual in this category because it does not put a daily cap on messages for base models — you can talk as long as you like, and what you give up is depth, not volume: shorter memory, shorter replies, fewer stored messages, no group chat. Paid tiers unlock the larger models, longer context, multi-character scenes, voice, and persistent history.

It is an adults-only platform. Access to mature content sits behind an NSFW toggle and an age declaration, and the site is explicit about who it is for. If you want a filtered, family-safe chatbot, this is the wrong tool and you should use something else.

How to start a CrushOn AI chat

Three steps from landing here to your first scene.

  1. Sign up free

    Create an account with an email address. The free tier includes unlimited messages on free models plus 100 monthly credits to test-drive Pro models — upgrade only when you actually need more.

  2. Pick your character

    Browse 8M+ characters filtered by tag, genre and gender, or skip browsing entirely and build your own: name, personality, backstory, voice.

  3. Start chatting

    Just start typing. Your character remembers what you said, adapts to your tone and builds on past scenes instead of resetting. History syncs across devices automatically.

Privacy & safety on CrushOn AI

CrushOn AI states that conversations are private to your account and are not used to train models. The NSFW layer sits behind an age declaration, and adult content is gated rather than served by default. That is the platform’s position.

The honest caveat: those privacy claims have not been independently audited, and this is the most criticised area in every third-party review we read. If discretion matters to you, the standard hygiene advice applies and costs you nothing:

  • Use a dedicated email address rather than your primary one.
  • Never share your real name, address, workplace or photos of yourself.
  • Treat anything you type as persistent — assume it is stored, even if the platform says otherwise.
  • Use a virtual card if you do not want the charge descriptor on your main statement.

This is an adults-only platform. You must be of legal age in your jurisdiction. All characters are fictional; nothing on this site should be read as advice about real relationships.

CrushOn AI FAQ

The questions that come up most often before signing up.

Is CrushOn AI free to use?

Yes. The free tier gives unlimited messages on free models, 8K memory, 200 stored messages per character and 100 monthly credits to try Pro models. The main limitation: free chat history is deleted after 7 days of inactivity.

Is CrushOn AI safe?

Conversations are private to your account and the platform says chats are not used for training. However, those claims are not independently audited — this is the most criticised area across third-party reviews. Use a separate email, no real name, no photos.

Can I create my own AI character?

Yes. You define name, gender, appearance, personality, backstory, scenario, speaking style and example dialogue, then choose a voice. Characters can be public or private. Step-by-step instructions: how to create a character on CrushOn AI.

Does CrushOn AI allow NSFW content?

Yes. It is an adults-only platform with an NSFW toggle that unlocks the mature part of the library. You must be of legal age in your jurisdiction, and you control whether mature content is shown at all.

Which AI model should I use?

Claude Opus 4.7 for long-form storytelling, GLM 5.1 for worldbuilding, DeepSeek V4 Flash for sharp dialogue, Crushon Leo for unfiltered outgoing personalities. Switch mid-chat to compare — see the model table above.

How much memory does it have?

Free models run 8K, Standard 16K, Ultra up to 24K. In practice 24K lets a character recall a detail from more than 100 messages back. Our memory guide covers how to keep long plots coherent.

Is there a CrushOn AI mobile app?

The web app is fully responsive on phones and tablets, and an Android APK is distributed directly by the platform. There is no confirmed native iOS app — iPhone users should add the web app to their home screen. More detail in our APK and mobile guide.

Can I chat with multiple characters at once?

Yes. Group chat supports three to four AI characters in one scene and is available from the Standard tier upward.

Does CrushOn AI generate images or voice calls?

No. The platform is text-first. It does offer AI voice replies — 40+ presets across nine languages — but no live voice or video calls, and no image generation.

What languages are supported?

The interface and models support multiple languages including English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, German, French and Portuguese. Voice presets cover nine languages.

How does CrushOn AI compare to Character.AI?

CrushOn AI allows unfiltered mature roleplay and offers 13+ switchable models; Character.AI is strictly filtered with a more polished interface and native mobile apps. Full breakdown: CrushOn AI vs Character.AI.

What happens to my chats if I stop using it?

On the free tier, chat history is cleared after 7 days of inactivity. Paid tiers retain history. If you want a long-running story, that alone is the main reason to pay.

Why do I need credits if I already pay?

Advanced (Pro and Ultra) models consume monthly message credits on top of your subscription. Free models never do. This is the single most confusing part of the pricing — we break it down on the pricing page.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, account deletion is available through account settings. Export or copy anything you want to keep first, since deletion is irreversible.

Is CrushOn AI down or not working?

High-traffic periods occasionally cause slow replies. Before assuming an outage, check the status page and our troubleshooting guide for the common fixes.
